# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese-speaking learners of Mandarin using a personal, offline-first vocabulary companion on desktop web, iPad, and phone. They want short, repeatable retrieval practice that is easy to understand and rewarding to use.

## Product Purpose

Vocab Universe turns four course vocabulary sources into a searchable study space with skill-specific FSRS review and focused arcade practice. Each game should make one retrieval action clear, fast, and readable; progress and game rewards must support learning without overstating mastery.

## Brand Personality

Focused, warm, playful. The experience should feel like a small, crafted learning game: approachable enough for a beginner, precise enough to trust, and energetic without visual noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid uncanny oversized mascots, generic emoji-led interfaces, web-form layouts wrapped around a canvas, crowded pill-heavy HUDs, decorative effects that compete with the target word, and toy-like visuals that make the learning action harder to read.

## Design Principles

- Retrieval is the hero: the target word and the required answer are always the clearest elements.
- One game, one mental model: setup, play, feedback, and result should be obvious at a glance.
- Small character, strong action: the mascot supports the throw-and-hit loop without becoming the scene.
- Depth creates calm: background, midground, and foreground separate the play space instead of filling it with decoration.
- Game rewards motivate practice but never fake learning progress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support WCAG AA contrast, keyboard play on desktop, touch targets of at least 44px on touch devices, visible focus states, responsive desktop/iPad/mobile layouts, safe-area insets, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone for correct, wrong, or power-up state.
